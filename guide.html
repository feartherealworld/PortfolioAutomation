What is WealthOS

WealthOS is a self-hosted portfolio command center for Hyperliquid, built from three pillars:

⚡ RSPS Autopilot

Reads Prof Adam's RSPS (Relative Strength Portfolio System) signal at bar close, parses the allocations, and rebalances your Hyperliquid account — spot where a market exists, 1x perps otherwise, optional per-asset leverage.

📊 Wealth Analytics

Deposit-aware returns (TWR, XIRR), Sharpe, Sortino, volatility, max drawdown, and your performance charted against just-holding BTC or ETH.

🧪 Strategy Lab

Bridge any TradingView strategy via webhook into a paper-trading engine with its own equity curve. Forward-test with fake money until it earns real capital.

How It Works

Everything runs in the cloud on Modal (free tier) — your computer can be off. On a schedule the bot:

  1. Reads the latest signal from Prof Adam's Portfolio Signals channel via TRW's API
  2. Parses the allocation percentages (e.g. 16.7% SOL, 83.3% Cash)
  3. Compares them to your current Hyperliquid positions
  4. Rebalances if the signal changed — spot markets when available, 1x perps otherwise
  5. Sends a Slack execution report: fills, deviation from the 00:00 UTC open, slippage cost

During daytime hours a new signal triggers a Slack message with Approve / Dismiss links — you decide from your phone. Overnight (when signals actually drop) it executes autonomously.

Step 1 — Get Your TRW Session Token

The bot needs to read messages from the signal channel. TRW uses a session token for authentication.

1

Open TRW in Chrome or Brave

Navigate to Prof Adam's Portfolio Signals channel.

2

Open DevTools

Press F12. Click the Network tab, then Fetch/XHR.

3

Reload and find the token

Reload the page (F5). Click any request in the list. Under Request Headers find x-session-token and copy the entire value.

⚠️ Never share your session token. Copy it directly into the manager GUI (python manage.py). Do not paste it into Discord, AI chats, or anywhere else. Anyone asking for your session token is trying to scam you.

Session tokens are long-lived (days to weeks). The bot alerts you on Slack when it expires.

Step 2 — Set Up Hyperliquid API

The bot trades through an API wallet that acts on behalf of your main account.

1

Go to Hyperliquid

Open app.hyperliquid.xyz, connect your wallet, and deposit USDC. WealthOS uses Hyperliquid's unified account — spot and perps share one balance, and the bot activates unification automatically.

2

Generate an API wallet

Click More → API. Name it (e.g. "signal-bot") — unnamed wallets overwrite each other. Click Generate then Authorize API Wallet, validity 180 days (maximum), and sign.

3

Copy your keys

  • Private Key — shown after generation → HYPERLIQUID_API_PRIVATE_KEY
  • Master Account Address — your main wallet address → HYPERLIQUID_MASTER_ACCOUNT_ADDRESS
⚠️ The API wallet can place and cancel orders but cannot withdraw funds. Worst case, bad positions could be opened — revoke the key instantly at app.hyperliquid.xyz.

Set a calendar reminder to regenerate the key before the 180-day expiry.

Step 4 — Slack Notifications Optional

Slack is WealthOS's voice: signal detections, execution reports with fill prices vs bar close, approval requests, health alerts.

  1. Go to api.slack.com/messaging/webhooks
  2. Create your Slack app → From scratch → name it, pick your workspace
  3. Incoming Webhooks → toggle ON → Add New Webhook to Workspace

1

Install Python 3.12+

From python.org — check "Add python.exe to PATH" during install.

2

The Dashboard

Four tabs behind one login. Numbers count up as they load, panels cascade in, and everything works one-handed on a phone at midnight.

◈ RSPS — the autopilot

  • Health badges — TRW / HL / SLACK status at a glance, plus the current trading mode (Auto or Approval)
  • Metrics — account value, unrealized PnL, open positions, all-time PnL
  • Equity curve — toggle Actual vs Daily open to see your execution alpha loss; export CSV
  • Positions — spot/perp mode tags, entry → mark, PnL, funding APR on perps (paying or earning), and liquidation distance on leveraged positions
  • Signal panel — the latest allocations with animated bars
  • Leverage panel — flip any asset from spot to leveraged perp (use with care)
  • Actions — Force Rebalance, Health Check, Refresh, and the Halt Trading kill switch
  • Approval flow — pending signals appear as a pulsing amber banner with one-tap Approve / Dismiss; Slack links land here too

◈ Portfolio — your wealth, honestly measured

  • Hero — total portfolio value with True P&L (deposits can't fake it), Return on Capital, Time-Weighted Return, and XIRR
  • Risk tiles — Sharpe, Sortino, annualized volatility, max drawdown, best/worst day — all computed on a flow-adjusted index so deposits never register as gains
  • Chart modes — Value, vs Deposited (cost basis), Performance (your TWR vs BTC-hold and ETH-hold, all normalized to 100), and Drawdown (underwater curve)
  • Strategy allocation — designate what % of the portfolio each strategy should run; editable in place
  • Cash flows — deposits and withdrawals auto-detected from the Hyperliquid ledger, with per-deposit contribution attribution; manual entries supported

◈ SDCA — BTC Strategic Dollar Cost Averaging

The Investing Campus SDCA signal, living inside WealthOS: the EQM Rainbow (kernel-quantile valuation bands), Composite Risk blended from four toggleable indicators (price valuation, Sharpe, cost-basis P/L, on-chain), and the draggable Accumulation/Distribution curve that backtests your buy/sell intensity per risk level against lump-sum — plus Today's Model Action telling you exactly what the active curve says to do right now. Data auto-loads (embedded history + live Binance refresh). Display & research only for now — execution wiring comes with the multi-strategy allocator.

◈ Strategies — the TradingView bridge

Add a strategy (name, asset, direction, leverage, paper capital) and WealthOS gives you a webhook token and a ready-made Pine alert() snippet. In TradingView, create one alert on your script with condition "Any alert() function call" pointing at the webhook URL. Every long/short/flat flip is then tracked by the paper engine: all-in position simulation, marked-to-market equity curve, sparkline, and signal log per strategy card. Alerts are idempotent — a double-fired TradingView alert can't double-trade.

Paper mode uses fake capital and places no real orders — forward-test until the equity curve earns your trust. Live routing through the real execution engine arrives with the multi-strategy allocator.

◈ History — the signal archive lab

  • Backtest — replay the full RSPS signal history against Hyperliquid candles with your fee setting; total return, CAGR, max drawdown, win rate
  • Equity curve variants — signal-price fills vs daily-open fills vs your live recorded equity, merged view included
  • Kelly panel — per-asset Kelly-criterion allocation estimates with an adjustable fraction slider
  • Signal table — every parsed signal with allocation pills and day-over-day changes

Safety & the Kill Switch

  • Pause bot — one tap on the RSPS tab stops all new orders across every strategy while keeping positions as they are. Signals received while paused stay held and a banner shows on every tab. Nothing auto-resumes: you decide.
  • Stop & sell all — the emergency exit: pauses the bot AND market-sells every open position to USDC in one action, with a full Slack report of the fills. Use when you want to be flat now.
  • Parse guard — if allocations don't sum to ~100%, the rebalance aborts before any order is placed.
  • Order caps — minimum trade size ($10), maximum single order ($50k), 3% slippage limit on market orders.
  • Approval mode — daytime signals require your explicit approval by default.
  • Full audit trail — every execution reports to Slack with fills, bar-close deviation, and slippage cost.

Maintenance

TRW token expired

You'll get a Slack alert. Grab a fresh x-session-token (Step 1) → python manage.py → paste → Save & Deploy. Takes 2 minutes.

Hyperliquid API key expired

Keys last at most 180 days. Generate a new one (Step 2) → python manage.py → Save & Deploy.

Updating WealthOS

The dashboard tells you when GitHub has new commits: an amber Update available banner appears with the latest change and an Ignore button (stored server-side, so ignoring once silences that version on every device). To update, on any computer that has the repo and Modal access, just double-click update.py — it pulls the latest main and redeploys in one go. The cloud bot never updates itself: deploying requires your Modal token, which stays on your machines by design. Manual equivalent:

git pull
modal deploy modal_signal_bot.py

Or run python manage.py → Save & Deploy. Your dashboard URL never changes. After deploying, give it a minute before clicking dashboard actions — a warm container can serve one last request with the old code.

The bot is a package now (signalbot/ next to the entry file), and Modal bundles it automatically at deploy — so always update the whole repo (git pull or a fresh ZIP); copying modal_signal_bot.py alone is not enough.

Upgrading an old v1 (single-file) instance

Same procedure — get the current repo, install requirements, make sure the Modal CLI is authenticated to that instance's account, and deploy. v1 and WealthOS use identical Modal names (app signal-bot, state signal-bot-state, secrets signal-bot-secrets), so the upgrade is in-place: same dashboard URL, secrets kept, equity history carried over, new features initialize themselves. If that instance's .env predates the dashboard login, run python manage.py once to re-save secrets with a username and password.

Running a second portfolio

⚠️ One bot per Modal account. The app, state and secret names are fixed — deploying "another instance" inside the same Modal account replaces the existing bot, and a renamed copy would share its secrets and state (duplicate crons → double trading).

For each additional portfolio: create a separate (free) Modal account, authenticate it with modal token new, give it its own .env with that portfolio's keys, and deploy. Each instance is fully isolated — own URL, own history, own secrets. Switch accounts in the CLI with modal profile activate <name>.

Pause / resume everything

modal app stop signal-bot        # pause (crons + dashboard offline)
modal deploy modal_signal_bot.py # resume

To pause trading only while keeping the dashboard live, use the kill switch instead.

Run the test suite

pip install -r requirements-dev.txt
python -m pytest tests/

Dashboard authentication

Login is a POST form that sets an HttpOnly session cookie (30-day validity, revocable by changing your password). Credentials never appear in URLs, browser history, or logs. Only salted digests of session tokens are stored server-side.

Where your secrets live

  • Local .env — your machine only, git-ignored
  • Modal encrypted secrets — encrypted at rest, readable only by your own functions in your own Modal account
Rules:
Never share your .env file or paste tokens into AI chats, Discord, or any website.
Never commit .env to Git (the .gitignore prevents this).
If a key may be compromised, revoke the API wallet at app.hyperliquid.xyz immediately.

FAQ

Spot or perps?

Both. Assets with a liquid Hyperliquid spot market (BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE, …) are bought as real spot tokens. Assets without one (like PAXG) use 1x cross perps — economically identical exposure. Setting leverage > 1 on an asset switches it to a leveraged perp.

What does "paying 11% APR funding" on a position mean?

Perp positions pay or earn a funding rate hourly. The positions table annualizes the current rate and shows whether your side pays (red) or earns (green). Spot positions never pay funding — one reason WealthOS prefers spot.

Why don't my deposits show up as profit?

By design. WealthOS separates money you added from money the strategies made: True P&L, TWR, XIRR, and all risk metrics are computed on a flow-adjusted index. A $1,000 deposit moves your account value, not your returns.

What if the signal says "No change"?

The bot detects it, does nothing, and sends a confirming Slack note so you know it's alive.

What if a trade is below Hyperliquid's ~$10 minimum?

Small allocation slices on small accounts can fall under the exchange minimum and get skipped — the rest of the rebalance executes normally. This resolves itself as the account grows.

How do paper strategies become live?

Forward-test in paper mode until you trust the curve. Live order routing through the shared execution engine (with per-strategy capital slices) ships with the multi-strategy allocator — until then, paper strategies never touch real funds.

Can I stop everything temporarily?

Kill switch for trading only (dashboard stays up), or modal app stop signal-bot for a full stop.

What's the "Daily open" toggle on the equity chart?

It shows what your equity would be if every fill had happened exactly at the 00:00 UTC daily open. The gap between it and Actual is your execution alpha loss — slippage plus price drift between signal and fill.

Is this financial advice?

No. WealthOS automates strategies you chose. Understand the signals before automating them. Use at your own risk.
